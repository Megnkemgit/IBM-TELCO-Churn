I’m excited to share a machine learning project focused on customer churn in telecommunications.

Using the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset, I built a reproducible workflow covering exploratory data analysis, leakage-aware preprocessing, and a comparison of baseline and tuned logistic regression and decision tree models. I evaluated the models with precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC, and confusion matrices, with a focus on the trade-offs that matter when identifying customers who may leave.

A key takeaway from the project is that churn prediction should connect model performance to business decisions: retention costs, customer lifetime value, threshold selection, fairness checks, and ongoing monitoring all matter before deployment.
